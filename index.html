How to Use

  • Your hand's Fan score is the sum of the Fan of all matching features.
  • Indented features replace the parent feature — count the child only. Full Flush replaces Mixed Flush; Win by Kong Replacement replaces Self-Pick.
  • Unless stated otherwise, triplets and quadruplets are interchangeable.
  • Calculate your payout on the Payment Table below. A red ▲ marks the winning tile.
  • † marks hands whose rules vary by table — MCR or Taiwanese imports. Agree them before the first deal.
